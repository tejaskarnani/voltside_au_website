What kind of property is it?
Apartments aren't eligible through this pathway. Roof access is normally shared and controlled by the owners corporation, so an individual rebate application doesn't apply here. Continue and we'll explain the alternative.

This one's not for us — yet

Apartment buildings usually put the roof under the owners corporation's control, not any individual apartment owner, so we can't run an individual eligibility check the way we can for a house or unit.

Solar Victoria runs a separate Solar for Apartments stream that works through the owners corporation rather than one resident. Worth raising at your next owners corporation or body corporate meeting — that's the path that actually applies here.
