1. Who we are

Voltside [ABN to be inserted] operates an energy rebate eligibility assessment and referral service for households and businesses in Victoria. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Voltside.

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains how we handle personal information, in accordance with APP 1.

2. What we collect

We collect only the information we need to assess your rebate eligibility and, if you ask us to, refer you to an installer. This includes:

We do not collect sensitive information as defined in the Privacy Act, and we ask that you do not provide it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

3. How we collect it

Almost all of it comes directly from you, through the eligibility form on this website. Some technical information is collected automatically by your browser when you visit. We may also receive information back from a partner installer about the outcome of a referral you asked us to make.

We do not buy contact lists, and we do not obtain your details from data brokers or third party lead sellers. If you have not contacted us, we will not contact you.

4. How we use it

  • To assess which government rebate programs you may be eligible for
  • To estimate potential costs and savings, and to send you that assessment
  • To contact you about the consultation you requested, if you consented to that
  • To refer you to one accredited installer, if you consented to that
  • To send you updates about changes to Victorian rebate rules, if you opted in
  • To measure and improve our advertising and our service
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations

5. Who we disclose it to

Partner installers

If, and only if, you give separate express consent, we disclose your contact and property details to one accredited installer for the purpose of quoting your job. We will identify that installer to you before we send anything. We do not distribute your enquiry to a panel of installers, and our agreements prohibit partner installers from on-selling or re-disclosing your details.

Service providers

We use third parties to operate the business — including customer relationship management, email and SMS delivery, website analytics, telephony, and cloud hosting. They may access personal information only to perform those services for us and are bound to protect it.

Advertising platforms

We may share hashed identifiers (such as a hashed email address) with advertising platforms to measure campaign performance and to reduce advertising shown to people who have already contacted us.

Others

We may disclose information where required or authorised by law, to protect our legal rights, or in connection with a sale or restructure of the business.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union. Before disclosing personal information overseas we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the APPs, as required by APP 8.

7. Direct marketing and how to opt out

We send marketing messages only to people who have opted in. Every email contains a working unsubscribe link and every SMS contains a reply-to-stop instruction, as required by the Spam Act 2003. You can also withdraw any consent at any time by emailing [privacy contact to be inserted], and we will action it promptly. Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect a consultation you have already booked.

8. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including access controls, encryption in transit, and limiting staff access to those who need it.

We retain your information for as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and evidentiary obligations — in particular, consent records are retained so we can demonstrate compliance. When no longer needed, we destroy or de-identify it.

We maintain a data breach response plan and will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires it.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your progress through the form, measure traffic, and attribute enquiries to advertising campaigns. You can block cookies in your browser, though parts of the form may not work correctly if you do.

10. Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, at any time (APP 12 and APP 13). Contact us at [privacy contact to be inserted]. We will respond within a reasonable period, normally 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first. If we refuse access or correction, we will tell you why in writing.

11. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first at [privacy contact to be inserted] and we will investigate and respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address and the version number and date appear at the top.
